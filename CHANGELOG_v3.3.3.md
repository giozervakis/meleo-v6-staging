# MELEO v3.3.3

## Profile & logout
- Desktop profile button now opens a proper account menu.
- Direct links to role dashboard and account settings.
- Reliable logout action on desktop and mobile.

## Professional Performance
Live marketplace performance dashboard with:
- Impressions in professional cards/search results
- Profile visits
- Phone taps
- Requests received
- Unique clients with completed visits
- Verified booking reviews
- Profile-to-request and request-to-client conversion indicators

Counters are persisted server-side. Impression/profile-view events are session-deduplicated in the frontend; phone taps count actual taps. Requests, clients and reviews derive from booking/review data.

## Verified Reviews
- Review is available only after a booking is marked Completed.
- One review per completed booking.
- 1–5 stars plus optional comment.
- Professional average/rating count updates automatically.
- Public professional profile renders verified booking reviews.

## Public professional contact
- Phone and email are available on the professional profile.
- Phone CTA contributes to Professional Performance analytics.

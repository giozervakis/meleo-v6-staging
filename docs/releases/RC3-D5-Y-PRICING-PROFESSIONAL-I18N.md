# RC3-D5Y - Pricing and professional lifecycle i18n

RC3-D5Y localizes the professional/commercial surface that remained in App.tsx.

## Scope

- Professional lifecycle status labels.
- Shared professional price label and price note helpers.
- Pricing title, subtitle, recommendation label, billing interval, CTA states,
  checkout/subscription feedback, and premium disclosure.
- Fallback BASIC/PREMIUM plan features.
- Greek and English professional.pricing / professional.lifecycle namespaces.
- Dedicated D5Y self-test and CI gate integration.

## Validation

- D5X regression self-test
- D5Y self-test
- TypeScript typecheck
- Vite production build
- Full CI gate

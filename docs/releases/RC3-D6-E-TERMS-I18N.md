# RC3-D6E - Terms of Use i18n

RC3-D6E localizes the substantive Terms of Use body while preserving
the Privacy Policy and Cookies document bodies for later tranches.

## Scope

- Nine Terms of Use sections.
- Dynamic provider, VAT and address interpolation.
- Dynamic emergency number.
- Dynamic support contact.
- New legalTerms translation namespace.
- Dedicated D6E self-test and CI gate integration.

## Explicit non-scope

- Privacy Policy body.
- Cookies and local-storage body.

## Validation

- D6D regression self-test
- D6E self-test
- TypeScript typecheck
- Vite production build
- Full CI gate
- git diff --check

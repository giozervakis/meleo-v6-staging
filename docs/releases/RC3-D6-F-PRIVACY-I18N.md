# RC3-D6F - Privacy Policy i18n

RC3-D6F localizes the substantive Privacy Policy body while preserving
the Cookies document body for a later tranche.

## Scope

- Eight Privacy Policy sections.
- Dynamic provider, VAT, address and DPO interpolation.
- New legalPrivacy translation namespace.
- Dedicated D6F self-test and CI gate integration.

## Explicit non-scope

- Cookies and local-storage body.

## Validation

- D6E regression self-test
- D6F self-test
- TypeScript typecheck
- Vite production build
- Full CI gate
